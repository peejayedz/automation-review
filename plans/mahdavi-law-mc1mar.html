How every new lead gets followed up

LeadPro catches every digital lead and keeps following up by text and email until your team reaches them, then reminds them to sign once the Vine Sign agreement goes out. Your team still does what it does today: call, qualify on the first call, and sign.

Built in LeadPro, switched off until you approve · November 2026

Two jobs, clearly split

LeadPro does

  • Collects leads from your website forms, website chat, Facebook ads and tracked phone calls
  • Sends an instant thank-you text and email
  • Follows up for 4 days until your team reaches the lead
  • Sends signing reminders after the Vine Sign link goes out
  • Alerts Cyrus if an agreement is still unsigned after 4 days
  • Keeps checking in by email for about 3 months with leads you couldn't reach or who aren't ready yet
  • Emails Cyrus and your case manager the moment a client signs
  • Keeps every text and email in one inbox

Your team keeps doing

  • Calling every new lead
  • The consultation, on that first call
  • Sending the agreement through Vine Sign
  • Opening the case in FileVine
  • One new habit: moving the lead's card after each call

On this page, “messages” always means both texts and emails. No booking step and no double entry: moving the card is what tells LeadPro to start or stop messaging.

Your team's daily routine

  1. Open the board

    Every lead is a card showing the name, phone, email and where it came from.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · Your team

  2. Call every new lead, newest first

    Start with the Web Lead and Phone Lead columns. The lead already has a thank-you text and email. Can't reach them? Leave the card where it is: LeadPro keeps following up for you.

    Where: your phone · Your team LeadPro follows up

  3. Spoke to them? Move the card

    Drag it to Lead Contacted, or Lead Qualified if it's a case you want. The new-lead messages stop.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · Your team, one drag

  4. Not ready yet? Move it to Lead Thinking

    For a good case that wants time to think, with no agreement sent yet. LeadPro checks in by email for about 3 months so the lead doesn't go cold.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · Long-term follow-up starts

  5. Signed on the call? Move it to Agreement Signed

    Cyrus and your case manager both get an email with the client's details right away, and every follow-up stops.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · Cyrus + case manager emailed

  6. Sent the Vine Sign link? Move it to Agreement Sent

    The column is called "Agreement Sent – Awaiting Signature". LeadPro sends signing reminders until they sign, and alerts Cyrus on Day 4 if they haven't.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · Reminders start

  7. Answer replies

    Texts and emails leads send back appear in one inbox. Reply there, or call them.

    Where: Conversations · Your team

  8. Not a case or spam? Close it

    Move the card to Lead Rejected or Lead Referred, or set its status to Lost. Nothing more is sent.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · Your team

  9. Before you leave, check Agreement Sent

    Anyone who signed today? Move them to Agreement Signed so the reminders stop.

    Where: Opportunities → Lead Stages · End of day
